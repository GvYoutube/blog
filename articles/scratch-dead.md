## Edit as of 11/19/26 (GTA 6 day lmao):

codubee has confirmed to me, when I asked what the maintenance was about, it's just a quite literal server movement.

Here's what he said, stitched together:

"Our on-prem servers (about 2 dozen of them) have been physically in a room that is operated by MIT, but rented from a data center in downtown Boston. That room is on the 7th floor of that building. MIT has decided to downsize their footprint in that room and they asked lots of groups who have servers there to find new locations for them. In our case, we decided to rent our own racks in the same building, but on the 5th floor instead of the 7th. This downtime should be the final stage of moving everything into the new racks. I was working towards making the move seamless, so that no one would even notice the switch over occurred, unfortunately, we don't have the money to have a whole new set of equipment, and, it turns out that MIT's BGP routing puts the two floors tens of milliseconds apart because traffic is going all over New England, rather than just in the building, forcing me to have to do the switchover all at once."
